Read the lesson text

The heart wall has three layers. From outside to inside: epicardium, myocardium, and endocardium.

The epicardium is the thin outer layer. The myocardium is the muscular middle layer that contracts to help pump blood. The endocardium is the smooth inner lining of the chambers.

The heart sits inside the pericardial sac. A small amount of fluid between the pericardial surfaces reduces friction during beating. The surrounding sac is not a fourth heart-wall layer.
