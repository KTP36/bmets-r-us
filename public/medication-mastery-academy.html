What You Will Learn

Each mission combines focused lessons, knowledge checks, clinical scenarios, explanations, XP, and achievement badges.

Mission 1: Medication SafetyCore administration principles, error prevention, and safe medication practice.
Mission 2: Drug ClassesRecognize major medication groups and connect classes with common uses.
Mission 3: CardiovascularACE inhibitors, ARBs, beta blockers, calcium channel blockers, diuretics, nitrates, statins, and anticoagulants.
Mission 4: RespiratoryRescue and maintenance inhalers, corticosteroids, bronchodilators, oxygen safety, inhalers, and nebulizers.
Mission 5: EndocrineInsulin, hypoglycemia, metformin, GLP-1 medicines, levothyroxine, and corticosteroids.
Mission 6: GI & RenalAcid suppression, antiemetics, bowel medications, renal dosing, electrolytes, potassium safety, and dialysis timing.
Mission 7: Infectious DiseaseAntibiotics, stewardship, allergies, cultures, antivirals, antifungals, C. difficile, and sepsis warning signs.
Mission 8: Emergency & High AlertEpinephrine, naloxone, reversal agents, insulin, glucagon, concentrated potassium, vasoactive infusions, and pump safety.
