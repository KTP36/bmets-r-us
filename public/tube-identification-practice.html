Quick Reference

Light BlueSodium citrate • commonly used for coagulation testing such as PT/INR.
Lavender / PurpleEDTA • commonly used for hematology testing such as CBC.
GreenHeparin • commonly used for selected plasma chemistry testing.
GrayGlycolytic inhibitor system • commonly associated with selected glucose-related testing.
Red / Gold / SSTSerum collection • commonly used for many serum chemistry and serology tests.
Blood CulturesSterile collection system • used when blood cultures are ordered.
Important: Tube colors, additives, test requirements, and collection systems can vary. Always verify the tube label and follow the current laboratory/facility procedure.

Keep Building the Skill

Tube selection is only one part of specimen quality. Correct patient identification, fill volume, mixing, labeling, transport, and the laboratory's current collection instructions all matter.
