Learn the Sequence

The commonly taught venous order groups tubes by additive. Tube colors can vary by manufacturer, so always verify the collection system and your facility/laboratory procedure.

1. Blood cultures / sterile specimens — culture bottles or appropriate sterile collection.
2. Sodium citrate — commonly light blue; coagulation testing.
3. Serum — commonly red/gold/tiger-top depending on the collection system.
4. Heparin — commonly green.
5. EDTA — commonly lavender/purple.
6. Glycolytic inhibitor — commonly gray.
Why does order matter? The sequence helps reduce additive carryover between tubes, which can affect specimen quality and test results.
