3. Sweepstakes Period

The Sweepstakes begins September 8, 2026 at 12:00 a.m. Eastern Time and ends November 29, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time (“Sweepstakes Period”). Entries received outside the Sweepstakes Period are not eligible.

During the Sweepstakes Period, complete an eligible MedSkillBuilder quiz or Academy and submit the associated qualifying score or completion form using your name and a valid, active email address. Eligible MedSkillBuilder activities are available without charge. No purchase, payment, or fee is required to enter or win.

Use of multiple email addresses or identities to obtain additional entries is prohibited and may result in disqualification.

Eligible submissions will be deduplicated so that each eligible person and active email address appears only once in the drawing. Odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received.
