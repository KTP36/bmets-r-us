From ECG to heartbeat

Connect P, QRS, and T with the heart’s electrical activity.

About 4 minutes · Video + 5 questions · Free · No account required

1. Watch the lesson

Your learning goals

  • Identify what P, QRS, and T represent.
  • Distinguish electrical activation from contraction.
  • Connect each wave with the atria or ventricles.

The heart is an illustrative model. The ECG is a teaching schematic, not a diagnostic tracing or a precisely synchronized measurement.

Read the lesson summary

An ECG records electrical activity. The P wave represents atrial depolarization, or electrical activation of the upper chambers. Atrial contraction follows.

The QRS complex represents ventricular depolarization. The lower chambers electrically activate, then contract to pump blood.

The T wave represents ventricular repolarization: electrical recovery before another activation. Electrical activation comes before contraction.
