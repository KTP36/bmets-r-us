8-module radiography learning path

Module 1

Patient Care & Professional Practice

Patient identification, communication, scheduling, infection control, mobility, contrast safety, emergencies, documentation, and professional practice.

Module 2

Radiographic Anatomy & Positioning Foundations

Anatomic planes, landmarks, body habitus, positions, projections, central-ray direction, and positioning terminology.

Module 3

Radiation Physics & X-Ray Production

Atomic interactions, x-ray tube operation, beam production, attenuation, scatter, filtration, and generator concepts.

Module 4

Exposure Factors & Image Acquisition

kVp, mAs, SID/OID, grids, AEC, digital detectors, exposure indicators, technique changes, and image quality.

Module 5

Radiation Biology & Protection

Biologic effects, dose, ALARA, pregnancy, collimation, shielding principles, occupational monitoring, and repeat reduction.

Module 6

Chest, Abdomen & Extremity Procedures

Patient-centered positioning, anatomy demonstrated, central-ray placement, breathing, trauma adaptations, and image criteria.

Module 7

Spine, Pelvis, Skull & Advanced Procedures

Axial skeleton positioning, trauma modifications, mobile/surgical imaging, fluoroscopic concepts, and advanced procedure decisions.

Module 8

Image Evaluation & Clinical Judgment

Systematic image critique, positioning error recognition, exposure analysis, artifacts, corrective action, and integrated clinical decisions.

Built to challenge you

Modules require 85% mastery. Questions emphasize application, plausible distractors, balanced answer choices, positioning decisions, exposure tradeoffs, radiation protection, and image critique rather than obvious recall.

Educational scope: This Academy reinforces concepts encountered in radiography education and entry-level practice. It does not replace an accredited radiography program, required clinical competencies, ARRT eligibility requirements, certification, state licensure, or clinical supervision.
