Think beyond memorization

Connect every structure to three ideas:

  • Location: Where is it?
  • Function: What job does it perform?
  • Relationship: What connects to it?

Quick check

Which chamber pumps oxygenated blood into systemic circulation?

Show answer and explanation

Left ventricle. It sends oxygenated blood through the aorta to the body. The right ventricle pumps blood toward the lungs.

High-yield anatomy reference

A concise review of commonly tested structures—without repetitive filler.

Aorta: Largest artery leaving the heart.
Alveoli: Lung sacs where gas exchange occurs.
Cerebellum: Coordinates movement and balance.
Diaphragm: Primary breathing muscle.
Gallbladder: Stores and concentrates bile.
Kidney: Filters blood and helps form urine.
Ligament: Connects bone to bone.
Tendon: Connects muscle to bone.
Ureter: Carries urine to the bladder.
Urethra: Carries urine out of the body.
Trachea: Conducts air toward the bronchi.
Ventricle: Lower pumping chamber of the heart.
