What real programs include

What Surgical Technology School Actually Teaches

A strong program combines classroom science, hands-on skills laboratory work, and supervised clinical experience. This Academy helps you build the academic foundation and decision-making mindset before and alongside formal training.

🧠
Classroom ScienceAnatomy, terminology, pathophysiology, microbiology, pharmacology, anesthesia concepts, and perioperative theory.
🧤
Skills LaboratoryScrubbing, gowning and gloving, sterile setup, draping, instrumentation, counts, positioning, and equipment practice.
🏥
Clinical ExperienceSupervised participation in real surgical cases, specialty exposure, teamwork, workflow, and progressive responsibility.
✂️
Instrumentation & TechnologyInstrument families, safe handling, electrosurgery, endoscopy, power equipment, robotics, imaging, specialty systems, and anticipation.
🧪
Sterile Processing & AsepsisPoint-of-use care, cleaning, inspection, packaging, sterilization monitoring, storage, surgical asepsis, contamination recognition, sharps, fire, and patient safety.
📋
Perioperative WorkflowPre-op preparation, intraoperative responsibilities, specimens, counts, closure, handoff, turnover, and professional practice.

Learn the OR by Seeing How It Fits Together
