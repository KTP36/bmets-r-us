What This Practice Test Helps You Study

CBET prep should connect definitions to real equipment behavior. The goal is not just memorizing words; the goal is recognizing what is happening in a circuit, a device, or a troubleshooting scenario.

Electrical Safety

Grounding, leakage current, safety testing, and basic electrical risk concepts.

Patient Monitoring

ECG, SpO₂, NIBP, patient monitors, alarms, sensors, and signal problems.

Therapeutic Devices

Infusion pumps, defibrillators, ventilators, suction, electrosurgery, and function-based questions.

Electronics Basics

Voltage, current, resistance, Ohm’s law, capacitors, rectifiers, switches, fuses, and circuits.

Troubleshooting

What to check first, how to narrow the fault, and how to think through symptoms logically.

Preventive Maintenance

Inspection, testing, documentation, performance verification, and quality control basics.

Study tip: Take the test first, review what you missed, then study the exact topics that caused the mistake.

Quick Sample CBET Questions

Here are a few examples of the kind of thinking you should practice. The full interactive section gives you a better test-style flow.

1. Which measurement is commonly used to verify electrical safety leakage current?

A. Volts   B. Microamps   C. Watts   D. Hertz

Answer: B. Microamps
2. A patient monitor repeatedly loses ECG signal. What should be checked first?

A. Printer paper   B. Battery charger fan   C. Electrode placement and lead wires   D. Nurse call system

Answer: C. Electrode placement and lead wires
3. Which component stores energy before shock delivery in a defibrillator?

A. Speaker   B. Capacitor   C. Fuse   D. Relay coil only

Answer: B. Capacitor

How to Use This CBET Practice Test Effectively

1. Take a baseline

Do the first attempt honestly. Do not look up answers. You need a real starting point.

2. Review misses

3. Retake weak areas

Repeat practice until your score improves consistently, not just once.
